TV Tracker privacy policy

TV Tracker is a personal app used by two people. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When you connect your Google account, the app asks for permission to read and edit Google Sheets. It uses that permission only to read and update one spreadsheet that its owner has shared with you. It does not open, read or change any other file in your account.

What it stores

The app has no server and no database of its own. Your Google access token is kept in your browser's local storage until it expires (about an hour), so you don't have to sign in on every visit. Everything else lives in the shared spreadsheet.

What it shares

Nothing. Show searches are sent to The Movie Database (TMDB) to find titles. No personal information is sent to anyone else.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
