    Reading the map

    Positions use the archive's host-star distance and Galactic coordinates, projected as d cos b. Error bars are not drawn; each planet's card gives the distance uncertainty and its source.

    Planet radius, mass and period come from the archive's composite table. It fills gaps with estimates (for example, radii inferred from mass), so check the archive overview before relying on one.

    Dashed white rings enclose 50% and 90% of the planets currently shown, measured in the plane. They update when you hide or show groups.

    Spiral arms are the log-periodic fits of Reid et al. (2019), with R₀ = 8.15 kpc. Bands are ±1σ arm width. Solid where maser parallaxes constrain the arm, dashed where extrapolated. The bar and the disc glow are schematic.

    Constellation figures join their stars at their true distances, so you can see how far apart in depth each familiar shape really is.
